Performance-driven TikTok ads with a sales focus. Two of the campaigns were featured as Top Ads in the TikTok Creative Center – TikTok's own showcase of high-performing ads.

## Execution

Hook variations, clear benefits, fast-paced cuts and iterative testing of openings and CTAs.

## Result

Featured in the TikTok Creative Center as Top Ads – twice. What made them work: [What two TikTok Top Ads taught me](../blog/tiktok-top-ads-learnings.html).

## Videos

The ads and further clips are on the [short-form video page](video-content.html).

## What I took from it

Content that felt like organic UGC performed best. The less it looked like an ad, the better it sold.
